# The PM Automation attendee pack

Everything from today's session, free: every prompt, the /goal templates, all 12 tested loops, the 20-task table as a one-pager, and the definitional table. It's yours whether or not you join the cohort.

## Your 15-minute first move

Don't read the whole pack today. Do this instead:

1. Open `prompts/morning-brief-routine.md`.
2. Copy the prompt into a new Routine at claude.ai/code/routines.
3. Attach only Google Calendar and Gmail. Detach everything else.
4. Click **Run Now**. Watch it finish.
5. Check Slack. Read what came back.

That's the whole move. One scheduled task, built and proven in 15 minutes. Everything else in this pack is the same shape, scaled up.

## How to use it

Start with the 15-minute move above. Once one scheduled task is running, come back and pick your next row from `20-tasks-map.md`. Most PMs build a loop second, since that's where the memory problem from primitive 1 gets solved.

This is the primer. Cohort 4 goes deeper: landpmjob.com.
